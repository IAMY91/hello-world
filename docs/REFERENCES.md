## AIRFLOW production conversation

The series opens with a framework overview. Each subsequent 45–60 second family episode shows the icon, names the family’s four patterns, and works through one practical example. Close with a concrete observation or question that helps a viewer try the language. The existing episodes are in English with captions.

For motion, keep Remotion as the composition and rendering base. Use frame-driven GSAP for icon motion and take scene-planning cues from Video Shotcraft. Attention starts with competing requests, brings the three currents together at the gold focus point, and gives routine and urgent requests separate routes in the Protected Focus example. The final check considers the report, the urgent response, and the backup’s workload.

The overview uses its original synthetic English voiceover. Attention uses local Qwen3-TTS with Ilya’s supplied recording and a presence-focused master. New episodes should time motion and captions against the selected read. The demo videos illustrate proposed arrangements. Describe observed outcomes only when a documented application supports them.
